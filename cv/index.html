Projects

  • Jul 2026
    DuplexTutorBench: A Benchmark for Full-Duplex Spoken AI Tutors
    Accepted at IEEE SLT 2026, first author
    • Built the first benchmark for full-duplex spoken AI tutors, in which any real-time speech model (GPT Realtime, Gemini Live, PersonaPlex, MoshiVis) tutors a simulated student on MathVista math problems.
    • Designed a two-track evaluation suite that scores speech quality with Audiobox Aesthetics, compares turn-taking timing against human conversation (Map Task corpus) using word timings from Whisper and forced alignment, and rates teaching with an LLM judge on 8 pedagogical dimensions. Benchmarking showed that no current model does both: real-time APIs teach well but take turns unnaturally, while full-duplex models time their turns much more like humans but teach poorly.
  • Dec 2025
    TEXT2SPACE: Enhancing LLM Reasoning Capabilities via Structured Output Learning
    Accepted at COLM 2026
    • Co-developed “TEXT2SPACE”, a novel spatial reasoning benchmark that utilizes ASCII visualizations to decouple representation failures from reasoning errors, identifying a “Read-Write Asymmetry” where LLMs interpret spatial layouts effectively but struggle to reconstruct them.
    • Fine-tuned Qwen3-30B models using LoRA and Hugging Face TRL on NVIDIA A40 GPUs, implementing “Learning to Draw” (text-to-ASCII) objectives to enforce the internalization of verifiable spatial “cognitive maps” during training.
    • Demonstrated reasoning transfer by validating that auxiliary training on structured spatial construction generalizes to improve downstream text-only reasoning accuracy by ~9% and enhances performance on external benchmarks like STEPGAME.
